The Instrument
CASE FILE / SELF-ASSESSMENT SUITE
Two readings, one sitting

Measure the mind's settings, not just its mood.

A disposition reading (in the style of the Myers–Briggs framework) and a reasoning workout, each scored with plain findings: what to keep doing, and what's worth working on.

Self-report instruments give a mirror, not a verdict — they describe tendencies you already half-know, phrased so you can act on them.

FILE 01

Disposition Reading

32 statements across four axes — how you direct energy, take in information, decide, and structure your days. Ends in a 4-letter type with strengths, blind spots, and upkeep notes.

Reasoning Workout

16 timed problems — numerical, verbal, and logical. Scored into a band with a category breakdown so you know exactly where to sharpen up.

On accuracy: this is a self-report personality instrument and an informal reasoning quiz, built for reflection — not a clinical or professionally normed psychometric assessment. The reasoning score is not a validated IQ. Treat both as a starting point for noticing patterns, not a fixed label.
